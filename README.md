Analyst Memo — Shopify App Store Insights
Dashboard: Shopify App Store Analysis

Reporting Period: Latest available data

Key Insight
The company have 485 apps, 196 with free plan and 289 wihtout free plan. Since 2018 the rating of reviews remains in average 4.20.
The total reviews in the period 2018 - 2023 was 7980. The users with pay made the major of reviews (4981).

Business Impact
The SEO category has 34 paid applications and the marketing category has 19 free applications. This means that the company has focused its developments on paid applications.

Recommendation
I recommend that the company increase the launch of new paid and free applications, because since 2018 the quantity has decreased, going from 46 to 17 free applications and from 53 to 33 paid applications.
